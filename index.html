About

I am a service and DevOps architect based in Akaa, Finland. My work history is on LinkedIn, but the short version is that I have worked in IT since the early 2000s, and I started building and playing with computers in the 1980s and 1990s.

My homelab is my test bench, my learning site and my own small production environment. A couple of cheap machines run a K3s cluster with dozens of apps, persistent storage on a NAS and every workload delivered by GitOps. I try to keep things simple, but it is a full environment, so I can keep my job skills sharp. I keep the important parts safe enough that breaking something does not affect anything I rely on.

I am not a developer by trade, but I build my own services and automations. The agentic environment is there to make that work more efficient: narrow mandates, explicit permissions, and a verification ladder that starts at the first file write.

Money is tight, so I spend about twenty dollars a month in total across a couple of AI providers. That buys some redundancy if one is down or its token budget runs out, and it means the harness has to stay efficient. Much of the work is improving my agent profiles, skills and tools so I get as much done as possible from that budget. So far it seems to be working. Everything described here runs in my own environment.
